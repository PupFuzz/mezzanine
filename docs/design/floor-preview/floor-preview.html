It is rendered from the artifact's own D3_SCOPE table rather than re-typed, and tools/design/floor-preview.selftest.mjs set-differences that table against § 5.4's own six surfaces in both directions on every run — so a surface D3 gains with no row reds the gate, and a row for a surface D3 does not publish reds it too. Not implemented is a ruling and not a gap: the floor is built once, by card #7341.

This round: a whole building: zoom out (⌂ or wheel) to see every floor stacked in cross-section, zoom in to one floor, and ride the elevator — click it, or a floor button — to travel between them. A larger floor you can zoom into — the meeting room, the lounge, the library corner all reward a closer look, and the vector art stays crisp at any depth. Characters are now individuals: body shape, pattern, ears, sprout, eyes, mouth, accessory and posture are each drawn independently from the seat's seed, and every creature carries a seeded vibe (shown in its drill-down, labeled as flavour — never wire data). Interns seed from their parent seat, so they differ too. The thought bubble is the desk's one rendered form of task — no chip beside it, nothing at all when task is null, and none over an empty chair, because it is anchored to a character. It never animates: a bubble that faded out on a timer would make no bubble mean no task or the timer expired (docs/design/FLOOR.md § 5.1). The windows and the wall clock step on each delivered feed.heartbeat — dawn, day, dusk and night skies, hands at minute resolution and no second hand — so when the feed dies they freeze with the rest of the page, and a stopped clock is the feed-down claim in the form every viewer reads without being told. Try it: switch the simulated feed to down and watch the clock hold while the poll count climbs; switch to never live and the room is drawn unset rather than at a plausible time. The viewer's clock is the value the heartbeat samples, never the thing that drives it (docs/design/FLOOR.md § 6.2 A17, § 6.5).

The communication layer (staged, card #7897): an open thread glows between participants with a bead per round (#725 shows four); carriers are shaped by kind — paper plane for a query reply, a folder handed to the coder intern; a broadcast is one radial pulse, never N arcs; the escalation flare is staged on the sola floor (a blocked seat raises a tethered light to the status wall until a human answers); and convergence is visible — "zero open questions" becomes a spark that files itself onto the wall board.
