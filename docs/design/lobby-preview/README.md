# Lobby-preview — the reference drawings for card#12113

**Operator instruction, 2026-10-20** (card#12113 comment 12527): *"there is not final G drawing. Do what you suggest to get desired result"* — the seat finished concept G and drew the lobby door view, and keeps both here as the reference the lobby build is checked against.

The two files are the final artboards of the *Lobby concepts, round 2* design canvas (https://claude.ai/artifact/PgWpQHryNjqpZEguueUgnh), copied byte for byte:

- `Lobby2G.dc.html` — **G, the lobby**: the room seen from inside, facing the elevator wall; the elevator as the control; the floor directory beside it with one plaque per floor, drawn at both ends of the scale (a floor at the marker cap and a floor of one agent); the building's readings under the board.
- `Lobby2H.dc.html` — **H, the cab's doors open at the lobby**: the cab seen from the rider's lens with the button panel, and through the open doors the lobby looking toward the building's front door and windows.

They are Design-canvas component files (`.dc.html`): they render inside that canvas's runtime, not as standalone pages, and their sample values are illustrative. **What the lobby must draw, read and say is `docs/design/FLOOR.md` § 4.1 and § 4.5's** — where a drawing and the spec disagree, the spec wins and the drawing is the defect.
