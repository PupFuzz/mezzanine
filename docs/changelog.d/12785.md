Each character's breaks follow one chain drawn from its name and the day, so every viewer and every reload see the same break at the same second with the same line; a page loaded at any time of day works its breaks out from that day's first break. A working agent's desk keeps its state chip, its lit monitor and its task bubble while its chair is empty; an idle one carries no food thought. An agent that raises its hand for attention, or goes stalled or unknown, while on its break walks straight back; a change from idle to working, or from one kind of work to another, lets the break finish. When two characters are already on their breaks as a page loads, each stands where a viewer watching throughout saw it stand.
