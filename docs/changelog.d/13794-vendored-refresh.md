- **card#13794** — **Every PR body now declares where its change came from, and the PR-body check judges it with the linter already on the target branch.** A PR body needs an `Origin:` line (`bin/change-pr-body.py --origin` writes it, with `--design-approved` for an agent-originated change), and a PR that edits `bin/pr-body-lint.py` is judged by the base branch's copy. The PR-body linter, its selftest and the dependency-advisory audit follow coord 0.77.0: the audit reads comma-separated advisory ranges whose pieces share no version as either-or, exports its affected rows for the bump job, and names a 404 or 403 on a reviewed-record lookup. Automatic dependency bump PRs are switched off: the nightly audit still checks both branches and reports every advisory, and its bump step records `BUMP PRS: OFF` and opens no PR.
