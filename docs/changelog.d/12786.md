- **card#12786** — **Each floor drawn from the shipped default room has a restroom door on its right wall, under the snack tables, and every agent and subagent, working or idle, takes a restroom break at a random time one to five hours after its last one, with *Taking a restroom break* in a thought bubble over its head from the moment it leaves its desk until it sits back down.** The operator asked on 2026-11-02 for a restroom door on the same wall as the snack area, visited at random one to five hours after each walker's last restroom break, with that status in its thought bubble, and answered the four design questions on 2026-11-02 as recommended. The walker walks to the door, stands at it while the door opens, goes in, and the door closes behind it; the sign on the door turns from a green ring (vacant) to a red bar (occupied) for the one-and-a-half to two-and-a-half minutes it stays inside; then the door opens, the sign turns back, and the walker walks home as the door closes behind it. One walker uses the door at a time: a walker whose break comes while the door is in use waits beside it, and the door serves walkers in the order their breaks began; past the last free waiting place, a walker skips that break. While a walker is away its desk keeps showing its state, monitor, badges and task bubble with the chair empty, and a subagent's stool shows empty. A walker whose seat asks for attention, or goes stalled or unknown, during its break comes out of the door first if it is inside and walks back at once; one whose agent leaves the floor, or a subagent that ends, comes out and walks to the elevator. A restroom break that comes due during a snack break starts at the snack table when the snack ends, and a snack break that comes due inside the restroom starts at the door as the walker comes out. Breaks start while the status strip reads live, and under reduced motion every character stays at its desk and the door stays closed with its sign vacant. To make room, the shipped default room's reading nook is trimmed: the right-hand floor lamp, the narrow plant, both cushions and the book pile are removed, and the armchair moves under the bookcase in the left strip; no desk moves and no agent changes desk. The floor tileset carries a new tile, `restroom-door`: a room map you author has a restroom where it places one, and a room with none has no restroom breaks. A desk inside a room with no open route to its door writes one line to the floor's event log: *`<room>/<seat>`: no open route between its desk and the restroom door — drawn without the trip*.
