- **card#13117** — **The admin console creates, names and retires rooms, and places each seat in a room from the Agents page.** The new *Rooms* page (`/admin/rooms`) creates a room with a key typed once and a name, optionally on a floor of the current building layout in the same act; renames it; and retires it once no live seat is in it and no coordination binding is bound to it, taking it off its floor in the same act. Each live seat on the *Agents* page has a *Room* control that moves it to a live room or back to *Unassigned*: every connected floor redraws the desk in its new room, the seat keeps its character, the desks around its old slot move back into place as after a retirement, and the move is recorded with who made it. The page warns, and does not refuse, when a moved seat declares an agent name another seat in its new room declares, and when it leaves a room a coordination binding is bound to. The building layout editor now refuses a save made after the layout changed since the page was opened, naming both revisions, and refuses a layout that places a retired room; issuing a coordination hook for a retired room is refused.
