- **card#12513** — **An agent walking to or from its desk takes the short way round: it steps back from its chair and walks off behind its desk, or leaves sideways past the desk's left end, and walks round its own couch and the subagents sitting on it rather than across them.** Before this, an agent whose desk had furniture against its left side could only leave along the line it sits on, out past the far end of its couch, so its walk to the elevator went the long way round and back along the couch. On the shipped default room that desk now walks to the elevator in about half the distance, and every agent's snack and restroom trips to its right walk round behind its desk instead of through its couch, so those trips take a few seconds longer. A desk in a room you author that is closed in on every side but its couch's still reaches the elevator, across the couch. Subagents walk as before. No map or desk changes.
