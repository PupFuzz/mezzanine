- **card#13117** — **The admin console names floors, renames them, sets their theme and places rooms on them, on a new Floors page.** The *Floors* page (`/admin/building`) creates a floor with a name, a theme and its first room; renames a floor, keeping its old address as a former address that still opens it, or taking an address over from another floor that used to have it, which the name field shows before the save; sets or clears a floor's theme; forgets a former address; and places a room on a floor, taking it off the floor it was on. A floor left with no room is removed and its addresses open nothing, so a new floor is refused the only room of another floor — rename that floor instead. Placing a room on a floor laid out with positions clears that floor's positions and hallway, and so does creating a room on such a floor from the Rooms page, which was refused before; the earlier layout revision keeps them. The room-map page is now labelled *Room maps*. A console action on a stored layout this build's reader refuses is refused by name, pointing at the building layout editor.
