The elevator's surround on the floor now carries two button plates in the floor's theme, the main one where it was and a smaller one directly under it. An agent stands in front of the main plate and a subagent in front of the low one, each pressing for one second, then walks to its spot by the doors. Walkers who reach the buttons close together press one at a time: a walker whose turn has not come waits beside its desk, or at its stool, until the one before it has pressed and stepped away. The random wait of up to 30 seconds now starts when the walker has pressed the button, so the walk from the buttons to its spot is part of it, and the doors open no sooner than the walker is at its spot. Waiting spots stay clear of where walkers stand to press. A subagent whose work ends on its way in also walks back by the low buttons and presses them. A desk whose route to the buttons is closed walks straight to the doors as before. Arrivals, the boarding order, the places inside the elevator and the doors' timing are as before. The buttons do not light up on a press, and the lobby's elevator keeps its single plate.
