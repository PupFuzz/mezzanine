- **card#12013** — **Clearing two terminals on one seat within a few seconds of each other keeps each terminal's working subagent on the floor until it finishes.** `fleet-reporter` matches the two hook signals of a `/clear` by which came most recently inside a short window. When both terminals' `SessionEnd(clear)` arrived before either `SessionStart(clear)`, or both starts before either end, each signal had two candidates and took the latest, which crossed the terminals: one terminal's running subagent was moved to the other terminal's new session, and that session's next turn end closed it as aborted while it still ran. A signal with more than one candidate now matches nothing and counts `clear_pair_ambiguous`; each carried subagent stays under its own cleared session and closes at its own `SubagentStop`, or at the server's 60-minute dispatch ceiling. Clears in one terminal after another still match one to one. The reporter moves to `0.6.3` (`fleet-reporter/UPDATES.md`). The server's session-close sweep that runs when a session ends with its turn still open now has a test that it leaves a carried subagent's call open. D1 § 8.4 and § 9.3 state the rule.
