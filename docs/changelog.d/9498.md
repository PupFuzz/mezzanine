- **card#9498** — **A seat behind a proxy that needs a login, or a proxy on its own certificate, reports.** `fleet-reporter` registered the login in `proxy_url`'s userinfo as a secret and never sent it, so a proxy that requires one answered every connection `407`; the reporter treated that as a passing fault, retried forever and delivered nothing, and its health probe read not measured. It now sends the login, percent-decoded, as `Proxy-Authorization: Basic`, to the proxy alone. A `407` stops that site's lane until the flusher restarts on a corrected config, counted `config_invalid`, and `selftest` fails the site's `config_readable` naming `proxy_url`. If this seat sets `ca_file` AND uses an `https://` proxy, add the proxy's CA certificate to that file BEFORE upgrading — 0.6.6 verifies the proxy against `ca_file` only, and without its CA the seat stops reporting (`selftest` shows `not_measured`). The reporter moves to `0.6.6` (`fleet-reporter/UPDATES.md`). D1 § 3.5 and § 11.5 state the rules.
