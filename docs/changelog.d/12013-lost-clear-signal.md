- **card#12013** — **A `/clear` that loses one of its two signals is counted, and the count shows in the seat's drill-down.** `fleet-reporter` matches a `/clear`'s `SessionEnd(clear)` and `SessionStart(clear)` inside a short window. When one of them never arrives, nothing pairs, and before this change nothing said so: a lost end was visible only as `clear_signals_paired` falling behind the seat's clears, a comparison nothing makes. The flusher now counts `clear_start_unpaired` for a start no end paired with and `clear_end_unpaired` for an end no start paired with, once each, after the half's window has closed. Matched clears count neither, and two terminals cleared together keep counting `clear_pair_ambiguous` alone. Both counters ride every heartbeat into the drill-down's reporter counters block. They measure whether lost `SessionEnd(clear)` signals are real, which decides whether the reporter needs a way to prove which session a lone start superseded; until then a lost end's calls keep today's bound, the server's session and orphan rules. What the floor shows is unchanged. The reporter moves to `0.6.4` (`fleet-reporter/UPDATES.md`). D1 § 8.2, § 8.4, § 9.3 and § 14 state the rule.
