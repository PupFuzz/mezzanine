- **card#12705** — **Agents and subagents leaving a floor wait at the elevator for up to 30 seconds, stand apart while several wait, all board together one at a time when the doors open, and the doors open and close at half their former speed.** The operator asked on 2026-10-31 for a random wait between 0 and 30 seconds (it was up to a minute), for waiting walkers to keep some space between each other instead of standing on one spot, for everyone waiting to go in one at a time before the doors close, and for the doors to move at about half their speed. A departing walker now waits at a spot of its own near the doors, on open floor and at least a walker's width plus its clearance margin from every other spot; when the doors open — for any walker, an arriving one included — every walker then waiting walks to the doors and steps in, nearest the doors first, and one that arrives while they are still open joins in; the doors close after the last. The doors take a second to open and a second to close (they took half a second); the step through them is unchanged, and the elevator ride the viewer takes is unchanged. Before this change each walker waited its own draw of up to a minute on the doorway itself, two waiting walkers were drawn on one point, and the doors opened for each walker's own wait.
