- **card#12964** — **A lost laptop or phone that is still signed in can be signed out: the recovery codes page has a "Sign out everywhere else" button.** It signs out every other browser and device signed in to the account, including any that ticked "Remember me", and the browser that presses it stays signed in. Moving to a new authenticator, re-enrolling or turning off the second factor now also signs out the account's other browsers, and an emailed two-factor reset signs out every one of them; a console password change already did. Sessions are ended in the database session store (`SESSION_DRIVER=database`, the shipped default).
